# Brief from the support lead

The rules-based assistant drafts replies for about 80 percent of requests and the team likes
the drafts for the simple cases. The problems are the ones it gets wrong quietly: a request
about a damaged order that gets a returns-policy draft, a billing question phrased in a way the
keywords miss, a customer who is clearly upset but uses none of the words on our list. Every
new phrasing means someone editing a keyword list.

We have been asked whether a language model could classify requests and write the first draft
from our knowledge base instead. Leadership wants an answer in three weeks, not a finished
product: is this worth building, and what would it take?

Things we care about, in order: no draft goes out without a person approving it; the
assistant should not invent policy that is not in our articles; upset or legal-sounding
requests must reach a person; it has to be at least as accurate as the keyword rules on the
requests we see today; and we need to know what it costs per request.

Things we do not need right now: a new inbox tool, connecting to the order system, handling
phone or chat transcripts.

Standard shipping arrives in three to six business days after the order leaves our warehouse. Express shipping arrives in one to two business days. The estimate shown at checkout counts from the shipping date, not the order date.

We ship to all fifty states and to Canada. Delivery estimates to Canada add two to four business days for customs processing, and duties are collected by the carrier at delivery.

Weather and peak seasons can extend delivery times. If a package is more than five business days past its estimate, contact us and we will trace it with the carrier.

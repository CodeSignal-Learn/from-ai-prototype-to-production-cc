category: returns_refunds

You can return or exchange unworn items with their tags attached within 60 days of delivery. Start the return from the Orders page, choose the items, and print the prepaid return label. Exchanges for a different size or color ship as soon as the carrier scans your return package.

Items marked final sale, and safety equipment such as climbing hardware, cannot be returned once opened.

Return shipping is free for exchanges. For refunds, a flat 7 dollar label fee is deducted unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

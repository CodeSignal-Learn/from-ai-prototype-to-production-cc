You can follow your order from the Orders page of your account. Once a package leaves our warehouse, the order shows a carrier name and a tracking number, and the carrier's page updates within 24 hours of the first scan.

If the tracking page has not updated for more than three business days, the package may be delayed at a carrier hub. In that case we can open a trace with the carrier; a trace usually resolves within five business days.

Orders placed before 2 pm on a business day leave the warehouse the same day. Orders placed later, or on weekends, leave the next business day.

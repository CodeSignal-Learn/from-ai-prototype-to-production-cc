title: Product care
category: product_issue
keywords: wash, care instructions, waterproof, re-proof, clean, storage, down jacket
---
Wash technical shells and down jackets on a gentle cycle with a technical-fabric wash, never regular detergent, and tumble dry on low. Down jackets need two or three clean tennis balls in the dryer to restore loft.

Waterproof coatings wear down with use. If water stops beading on a shell, wash it and apply a spray-on re-proofing treatment; this restores the coating without affecting the warranty.

Store sleeping bags and down items uncompressed in a dry place. Long-term compression permanently reduces insulation.

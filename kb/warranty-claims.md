Fernwood gear is covered against defects in materials and workmanship for two years from delivery. Zippers, seams, buckles, and waterproof coatings are covered; normal wear, abrasion, and damage from misuse are not.

To start a claim, send us your order number, a description of the problem, and two photos showing the defect. We reply within two business days with a repair, replacement, or store credit decision.

Repairs are free and take two to three weeks including shipping. If we replace an item that is no longer made, we offer the closest current model or store credit for the original price.

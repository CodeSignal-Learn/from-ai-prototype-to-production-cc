To reset your password, choose "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter the email address on your account. The reset link arrives within a few minutes and stays valid for one hour. If you do not see it, check your spam folder and confirm you used the email address the account was created with.

After five failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. Waiting out the lock and then resetting your password clears it.

If you use two-factor authentication and no longer have your device, we can remove the second factor after verifying your identity with the billing address and the last four digits of the payment card on file.

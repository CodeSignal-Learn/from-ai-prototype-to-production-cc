---
title: Merging or updating your account
category: account_access
keywords: merge, duplicate account, change email, update email, two accounts, delete account
---
If you have created two accounts by accident, we can merge them so that all orders and rewards points appear in one place. Tell us the email addresses of both accounts and which one you want to keep; the merge takes one business day.

You can change the email address on an account yourself from the Account settings page. A confirmation link is sent to the new address and the change applies once you open it.

To delete an account, contact us from the email address on the account. Deletion removes saved addresses and payment methods; order history is kept for legal record-keeping.

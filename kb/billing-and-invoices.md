title: Billing, charges, and invoices
category: billing
keywords: charged, charge, invoice, receipt, payment, double charge, pending charge, billing
---
When you place an order we authorize the full amount, and the charge settles when the order ships. A pending authorization and a settled charge can both appear for a day or two, which looks like a double charge; the pending one drops off automatically within five business days.

Receipts are emailed when the order ships, and you can download an invoice for any order from the Orders page.

If a charge appears that does not match an order in your account, contact us with the amount and date and we will investigate. Real duplicate charges are refunded within five business days.
